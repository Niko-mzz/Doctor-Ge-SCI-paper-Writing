# Submission and Peer Review

Treat the manuscript, journal instructions, and reviewer comments as controlled evidence.

## Initial Submission

1. Verify the target journal's current author instructions and required files.
2. Build a submission checklist covering manuscript, figures, tables, supplements, cover letter, highlights, declarations, authorship, funding, conflicts, data availability, and AI disclosure where required.
3. Cross-check titles, author names, affiliations, figure numbering, citations, and file references.
4. Draft submission materials from verified manuscript facts only.

## Revision

1. Split reviewer comments into atomic items and classify them as scientific, statistical, editorial, formatting, or policy-related.
2. Map each item to an action, owner, evidence, manuscript location, and status.
3. Revise the manuscript first, then write a point-by-point response stating what changed and where.
4. Track changed claims, data, figures, and citations and rerun affected checks.

## Output

Deliver the checklist, response matrix, revised-text map, cover letter or response letter, unresolved issues, and final compliance report. Never claim that a change was made when it was not.
