Covers the full paper lifecycle: Chinese draft ? English translation ? polishing ? submission. Tailored for rare-earth luminescent materials papers.

## 1. IMRaD Structure (???? ~7-10 pages)

| Section | Length | Key Rule |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Abstract** | ~250 words | Must include concrete data (peak positions, n values, CIE coordinates) |
| **Introduction** | ~1.5 pages | Funnel: big picture ? problem ? prior work gaps ? your solution |
| **Experimental** | ~0.5 pages | Concise; cite prior methods when possible |
| **Results & Discussion** | ~4 pages | 3-5 subsections, each driven by one figure |
| **Conclusions** | ~0.3 pages | Numbered points, no new data, mirror abstract |

## 2. Innovation Framing (?????)

When comparing your system to a prior one, use the "4-dimension comparison":

1. **Emission band**: Your new bands vs. prior limited range
2. **Sensitization**: Multi-step energy transfer vs. single sensitizer
3. **Color tunability**: Wider tuning range, fewer interfering peaks
4. **Transition mechanism**: Different photon process, bridging levels

Template: "Compared with [prior system] which [limitation], our [new system] achieves [advantage] through [mechanism]."

## 3. Abstract Formula

Sentence 1: Material + method (1 sentence)
Sentence 2: Key comparison with prior work (1 sentence)
Sentences 3-5: Quantitative results with numbers (3 sentences)
Sentence 6: Mechanism insight (1 sentence)
Sentence 7: Significance/implication (1 sentence)

## 4. Figure-Driven Writing

- Each subsection in Results = one figure
- Write figure caption first, then the paragraph
- Caption format: "Fig. X. [One-sentence title]. [Key conditions in parentheses]."
- Data tables: include for concentration optimization series

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Abstract without numbers ? generic and weak
- Introduction that lists facts without logical flow
- Results without comparison to prior work
- Conclusions that repeat abstract verbatim
- Missing energy level diagram (essential for UC papers)

## 7. Journal-Specific Tips

**Journal of Luminescence**: 
- IMRaD, no section numbering needed in final
- Figures embedded in text or at end
- ~15-30 references
- Highlights: 3-5 bullet points, 85 characters each
- Cover letter required

## 8. Quick Checklist

- [ ] Abstract has specific data values
- [ ] Introduction ends with "In this work, we..."
- [ ] Each figure has a corresponding subsection
- [ ] Energy level diagram explains all transitions
- [ ] CIE coordinates calculated and discussed
- [ ] Comparison with prior work in every results subsection
- [ ] Conclusions are numbered and quantitative
- [ ] All references cited in text
