# SCI Abstract Template (摘要模板)

## Structure (7 sentences)

```
Sentence 1: [Material] were [prepared/synthesized] by [method].
Sentence 2: Compared with [prior system], we [key innovation].
Sentence 3: Under [excitation], [main result 1 with numbers].
Sentence 4: [Main result 2 with numbers], optimal at [value].
Sentence 5: Power dependence reveals [n-photon process, n≈X].
Sentence 6: [Mechanism insight — energy transfer path, synergistic effect].
Sentence 7: These results demonstrate [material] is promising for [application].
```

## Common Keywords

- 上转换发光: upconversion (UC) luminescence
- 能量传递: energy transfer (ET)
- 敏化剂/激活剂: sensitizer / activator
- 浓度猝灭: concentration quenching
- 交叉弛豫: cross-relaxation (CR)
- 色坐标: CIE chromaticity coordinates
- 相关色温: correlated color temperature (CCT)
- 荧光寿命: fluorescence lifetime
- 声子能量: phonon energy
