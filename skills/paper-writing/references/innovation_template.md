## Defense-ready Soundbites (答辩金句)

Template:
1. "Compared with [prior system]'s single [band] emission, our [system] achieves dual-band [band1 + band2] emission, significantly broadening the spectral coverage."
2. "[Ion X] not only accepts energy from [sensitizer] but also relays it to [ion Y], providing secondary sensitization; meanwhile, cross-relaxation between [X] and [Y] creates unique intensity evolution patterns."
3. "Without interfering peaks from [prior ion], our system achieves purer chromaticity and wider color tuning range."

## Innovation Combination Strategies

- **Mechanism depth**: Innovation 2 + Innovation 1 + Innovation 4 (energy transfer + new bands + transition)
- **Application focus**: Innovation 1 + Innovation 3 + Innovation 5 (new bands + color + stability)
- **Abstract condensed**: Three most impactful quantitative claims
