# Academic Language Polishing

Improve language without changing the science.

## Workflow

1. Lock factual invariants: numbers, units, sample sizes, equations, identifiers, citations, and stated uncertainty.
2. Correct grammar, syntax, punctuation, terminology, tense, and agreement.
3. Reduce ambiguity, repetition, inflated claims, vague pronouns, and unnecessary nominalization.
4. Improve paragraph flow and transitions while preserving claim order unless restructuring is requested.
5. Flag sentences whose scientific meaning is unclear instead of guessing.
6. Check the revision against the original invariants.

## Output

Provide a polished version plus a concise change log. For sensitive passages, show original, revision, and reason. Do not add new results, citations, mechanisms, or claims.
