# Journal Selection

Verify all changeable journal information from current official journal or publisher pages.

## Workflow

1. Summarize the manuscript's field, contribution, article type, methods, audience, and constraints.
2. Generate candidates from scope fit and comparable published work.
3. Verify current aims and scope, accepted article types, formatting limits, access model, fees, and relevant policies.
4. Record indexing or metric claims only with a dated authoritative source; do not infer acceptance probability.
5. Score candidates transparently by fit, evidence strength, cost, timing, and risk.
6. Build a primary target and fallback submission cascade.

## Output

Return a ranked table separating verified facts from inference, with source links, access date, fit rationale, risks, and next actions.
